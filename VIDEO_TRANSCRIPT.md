# VoxTube — real case film

Edited actual browser captures and explanatory cards. Synthetic narration (macOS Daniel), not Petr's voice. Recorded selection, local review and save actions; static captures held for explanation. Frozen data, not a new collection. Nonessential quotations masked.

## 0:00 · A positive label. A negative reaction.

A positive label can hide a negative reaction. This real Ferrari Luce case shows why Petr built VoxTube: to turn public discussion into better research questions, without handing judgment over to AI.

## 0:13 · What should the launch team investigate?

After a launch, the problem is not finding opinions. It is deciding which ones deserve attention. A marketing team needs sharper questions before changing its message. An analyst needs the evidence behind those questions.

## 0:28 · Real discussion. A bounded sample.

The case uses seventy-five real top-level comments from one Hacker News thread. Collection used the official API. The discussion dates from May, collected in October. These are self-selected public commenters, not verified customers or a representative market sample.

## 0:46 · Find a signal. Keep the base.

VoxTube brings the sample into a filterable view. Fifteen of the seventy-five comments received both a negative label and the exterior-design theme. That is a useful place to start reading, not proof of what buyers think.

## 1:01 · Read past the label.

Here is the trap. The classifier marks this comment positive. But the apparent praise belongs to a Prius, used to criticise the Ferrari. Reading the wording changes the interpretation. A rival mention is not automatically approval or purchase consideration.

## 1:18 · Ask for a second reading.

The selected comment is sent to Gemma, running locally through Ollama. This is an actual model call captured in the interface. It returns a negative second reading and explains the comparison. The original classifier label and totals are preserved.

## 1:38 · A second model is not a referee.

That does not make the second model infallible. In the earlier three-case check, another comparison remained unresolved. Long-form summaries also misread irony. These were deliberately chosen diagnostic cases, not an accuracy benchmark. The analyst still has work to do.

## 1:56 · Keep the finding with its evidence.

The next step is an analyst note, not an automatic correction to the market score. This finding proposes testing whether the exterior feels distinctive enough for Ferrari. It is saved with the selected comment and the original scope. The evidence record was also downloaded.

## 2:13 · From a reaction to a research brief.

For the business, this becomes a focused brief: probe exterior appeal and brand distinctiveness, examine interior technology separately, then validate with relevant prospective buyers before changing launch messages. The case demonstrates a traceable workflow, not measured sales impact or time savings.

## 2:34 · An idea made usable. An answer kept honest.

Petr framed the problem, used AI coding tools to help build the application, and tested the result against real discussion. VoxTube connects analytical thinking with practical execution: find a useful source, build the workflow, and be clear about what the evidence can support.
